## About
Shrink Studio is a London Webflow Premium Partner for venture-backed B2B tech companies, from Seed to Series B. We build the site a raise deserves, then keep making it better.

We work in three ways. Discovery settles who the site is for and what it has to prove, in a written brief the whole team agrees on. Development turns that brief into a fast, component-built Webflow site your marketing team can run on its own. Optimisation carries on every month after launch, with conversion testing, answer engine optimisation and a clear report on what moved.

A small senior team does the work, from the first call to launch and beyond. Recent clients include Adfin, CloudNC, Forest, nxzen, Buff Motion and Steamhaus. In the two weeks after Adfin's new site launched, its visibility in AI answers rose from 5% to 22%.
