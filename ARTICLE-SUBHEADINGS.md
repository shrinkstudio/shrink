### Two cautions

- **Post 3 overlaps two articles you already have** ("Getting quoted by ChatGPT" and "Why your site ranks but never gets cited"). Write it as the how-to guide and link the other two from it as supporting pieces, so the three don't compete for the same question.
- **Orbit's advice is to stop writing pages aimed at the seven zero-volume phrases** (eg. "aeo agency for b2b saas"). The six posts above are aimed at questions buyers ask assistants, which is a different bet: judged by citations, with search traffic as a bonus.

### Not suggested

- "What's the difference between a design agency and a Webflow developer" and "is there a Webflow design system agency in London" are tracked questions, but they are better answered on the Services page and the Design Systems solution page than in a post.
